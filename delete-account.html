Delete your MyWalkMap account

How to request account deletion and what happens to your information.

This page applies to the MyWalkMap app. You can delete your account from inside the app. If you cannot access the app, you can contact us by email to request deletion.

Support email: kumaresankp21@gmail.com

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

  1. Open MyWalkMap and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Enter your current account password and type DELETE when asked.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

After the server confirms the deletion, your Supabase authentication account and associated cloud profile are deleted, and the app signs you out.

Option 2: Request account deletion by email

  1. Send an email from the email address registered to your MyWalkMap account.
  2. Use the subject line MyWalkMap account deletion request.
  3. In the message, ask us to delete your MyWalkMap account. Do not include your password.
Email an account deletion request

We will verify that the request relates to the account holder and process the deletion. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days. We may contact you if we need additional information to verify the request.

What information is deleted?

  • Your Supabase authentication account, including the account's email address and authentication credentials.
  • Your MyWalkMap cloud profile, including the name and avatar URL you provided.

Deleting the authentication account also removes its associated profile record through the app's account-deletion process.

What stays on your phone?

MyWalkMap currently stores workout history and GPS route records locally on your device. These records are not uploaded to your Supabase account by the current app design. For that reason, deleting your cloud account does not automatically erase locally stored workouts or routes.

To remove local MyWalkMap data, open Android Settings → Apps → MyWalkMap → Storage → Clear storage/data. The exact labels can differ by Android device.

Important: If you delete your account by email because you cannot open the app, your locally stored workouts and routes will remain on the device until you clear the app's storage yourself.

What may be retained temporarily?

Limited technical records or provider-managed backups may remain for a limited period under the relevant service provider's normal retention and deletion processes. They are not used to restore a deleted account.
