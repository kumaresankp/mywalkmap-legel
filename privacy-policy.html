Privacy Policy

Every Step Has a Story.

Effective date: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyWalkMap ("the app", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the MyWalkMap walking and running tracker.

Developer:Kumaresan K P

Privacy contact: kumaresankp21@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

Account and profile information. If you create a MyWalkMap account, Supabase Authentication processes your email address and authentication credentials. Supabase handles password authentication; MyWalkMap does not store your password in readable form. Your profile may contain your name and avatar URL, if you provide them. Supabase also assigns an account identifier to associate your account with your profile.

Location information. When you start a walking or running workout and grant location permission, MyWalkMap uses your device's location to record the route and calculate workout information such as distance, duration and pace. Location access is used for the tracking feature, not to build an advertising profile.

Workout information. Workout records and GPS route points are stored locally on your device using the app's local storage. In the current app design, workout history and route points are not uploaded to your Supabase account.

Guest Mode. You can use the app in Guest Mode without creating a Supabase account. Workout information created in Guest Mode is stored locally on your device.

2. Information stored on your device

MyWalkMap stores workout records and GPS route information locally on your device so you can view your activities and summaries. This local information is not part of your Supabase profile and is not uploaded to our Supabase database by the current app implementation.

Deleting your MyWalkMap account removes your Supabase authentication account and cloud profile. It does not automatically erase workout records or GPS routes already stored on your device. You can remove local app data using Android Settings → Apps → MyWalkMap → Storage → Clear storage/data. The exact wording may vary by device.

3. How we use information

  • To create and authenticate your account, restore your signed-in session and manage your profile.
  • To access device location during a workout and record the route shown in the app.
  • To calculate and display workout distance, duration, pace and activity summaries on your device.
  • To provide account deletion and respond to support requests.
  • To maintain, secure and troubleshoot the app.

We do not use your GPS route history to serve targeted advertising.

4. Service providers and sharing

Supabase. We use Supabase for account authentication and the cloud profile database. Account and profile information is sent to Supabase over HTTPS and is subject to Supabase's Privacy Policy. Supabase also handles account-related authentication emails, such as verification or password-reset messages, when those features are used.

MapLibre. The app uses the MapLibre mapping software development kit to display maps. MapLibre is the map-rendering component; map tiles are requested from the map tile provider described below.

OpenFreeMap. The current map style uses OpenFreeMap tiles at tiles.openfreemap.org. When the map loads, the tile service and its hosting infrastructure may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and requests for map tiles covering the area being displayed. Those requests can reveal the approximate map area viewed. See OpenFreeMap for information about the service.

We do not sell personal information. We do not provide your locally stored workout history or route records to Supabase as part of the current app design. We may disclose information if required by law or where necessary to protect the security and rights of users or the service.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and profile information while your account remains active. You can initiate account deletion from inside MyWalkMap using Profile → Delete Account. The app asks you to re-enter your password and confirm the request. The server then deletes your Supabase authentication account and associated profile record.

You can also request account deletion if you cannot access the app. See our Delete MyWalkMap Account page for instructions. We will process a verified request and delete the associated cloud account and profile information.

As explained above, account deletion does not automatically remove workout history and GPS routes stored locally on your phone. You must clear the app's local storage on the device separately if you also want those records removed.

Limited copies may remain temporarily in provider-managed backups or logs according to the relevant provider's retention and deletion processes.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect account and profile information. Connections to Supabase use HTTPS, and database access controls are intended to restrict profile access to the authenticated account owner. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Please use a strong, unique password and keep access to your email account secure.

7. Children's privacy

MyWalkMap is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent is required. If you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable requirements, contact us using the address below so we can review the request.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, service providers or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date on this page. For material changes, we may also provide a notice in the app.
